# Audience Retention Critic Skill

## Purpose
Evaluate whether an episode is compelling for viewers, not only correct.

## Responsibilities
- Review opening hook.
- Check curiosity progression.
- Identify likely drop-off points.
- Evaluate emotional and explanatory payoff.

## Position
Runs alongside Entertainment Critic after Story Engine draft.

Flow:
Story Engine
→ Audience Retention Critic
→ Entertainment Critic
→ Rewrite

## Output
- Retention risks
- Hook evaluation
- Scene-level improvement suggestions
