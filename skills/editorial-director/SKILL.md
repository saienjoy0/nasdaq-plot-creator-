# Editorial Director Skill

## Purpose
Act as the upstream showrunner decision layer.

## Responsibilities
- Decide whether a topic deserves an episode.
- Compare possible narrative angles.
- Select the strongest viewer-facing question.
- Preserve evidence and causal constraints.

## Position
Runs before Story Engine.

Flow:
News candidates
→ Editorial Director
→ Causal Research
→ Story Engine

## Output
- Why now
- Viewer hook
- Core question
- Selected angle
- Rejected alternatives
